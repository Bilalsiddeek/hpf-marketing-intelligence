# Brands Watchlist

Brands whose moves teach transferable lessons to **$3M–$10M founder-led, marketing-led and ethical consumer brands**. These are benchmarks for mechanisms, not HPF's competitors (those live in `agencies.md`).

**Tiers**
- **Tier 1**: searched by name on every run.
- **Tier 2**: searched once a week (Mondays) or when surfaced in discovery.
- **Tier 3**: only when they come up in discovery.

**Editing:** add, remove or re-tier freely. Keep "Why we watch" specific: it tells the agent what kind of signal matters for that brand.
