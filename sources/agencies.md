# Agencies, Competitors & Operators Watchlist

Three jobs:
1. **Competitive intelligence**: who HPF competes with for $3M–$10M consumer-brand clients, and how they position, package and price.
2. **Craft benchmarks**: agencies whose work sets the bar for strategy and creative.
3. **Operators and thinkers**: named people whose frameworks, data and takes shape what HPF's audience believes.

**Tier 1** entries are searched by name on every run; the rest weekly or on discovery.
